Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what the Ambient Lab app (“the app”) does with information. The app is published by Gregory Hilderbran Jr (“we”, “us”).

The short version: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect personal information. Your settings and saved mixes stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. It has no accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools from other companies. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not use your location.

Information that stays on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Your settings, saved mixes, widget choices and the scene you last used.
  • Scenes you have downloaded.
  • The Now Playing widget, if you add it, shows the title and artist of what is playing on your device. The app reads this from the system and does not send it anywhere.
  • If you start a free trial, the app may ask permission to send a reminder the day before it ends. The reminder is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can say no.

Your device backups, which Apple manages, may include these settings. You can clear your settings and saved mixes with “Reset All Data” in the app’s Settings, or remove everything by deleting the app.

Information handled by others

Downloading scenes. The app downloads its scene list, preview images and video files from our media server at media.ambientlabapp.com, which is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives technical details such as your IP address and which files were requested. The app sends no account, name or advertising identifier with these requests, and we do not use this information to identify you, build a profile of you or track you. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy governs how it handles that data. Files are downloaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and checked for integrity before the app uses them.

Purchases. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app asks Apple on your device whether Premium is active. Apple’s privacy policy governs that information.

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it for advertising. Apple and Cloudflare, as described above, are the only other parties involved.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep no personal information on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Information on your device is removed with “Reset All Data” or by deleting the app. Technical data held by Cloudflare or Apple is kept under their policies. Scenes already on your device keep working without a connection.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is generally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you live somewhere with data-protection rights (for example the EU, the UK or California) and want to make a request or ask a question, contact us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the current version is always on this page.
